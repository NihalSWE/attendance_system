Params: at (ask, order, receive, qc, return), doc (a document, optional). {% endcomment %}
1Ask optional 2Order 3Receive 4Quality check if needed {% include "ui/icon.html" with name="check" small=True %}In stock ↩Return to supplier

{% if at == "ask" %} Ask to buy. A department lists what it needs; an approver says yes or no. Approved lines become a purchase order (Purchase orders → From requisitions). Skip this step when nobody needs to approve. {% elif at == "order" %} Order from the supplier. One order per supplier: the items, how many and the price. It is approved, then sent (Email to supplier or PDF). Nothing comes into stock yet. {% elif at == "receive" %} Receive what arrived. A goods receipt (GRN) says what came into which store, against an order or without one. Posting it puts the stock in; items with a quality check wait until they pass. {% elif at == "qc" %} Check the quality. Pass a batch and it can be used; fail it and it is blocked, then sent back with a purchase return. {% elif at == "return" %} Send goods back. Failed the check, damaged or the wrong item: posting a return takes it out of stock at the receipt's cost. {% endif %} Buying something small without an order? Stock in → A purchase receives it in one step.
