Overtime

Time after the shift's end is overtime. When the person scans out it is approved automatically; on the Overtime page you can pay fewer minutes or reject a day, and set when they left if they never scanned out. The hourly rate of monthly staff is one day's pay ÷ the shift's hours; of daily staff, the daily rate ÷ the shift's hours.
