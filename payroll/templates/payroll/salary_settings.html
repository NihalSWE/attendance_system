Attendance bonus

Optional: a bonus for being present on every working day (or at least the days you set). Present days are Present (late included) and days without a check-out; a half day, any leave and an absence are not. Holidays and weekly offs are not working days. Nobody gets it for a month or week they joined or left in. It is added to the payslip after the penalties.

Overtime

Time after the shift's end is overtime. When the person scans out it is approved automatically; on the Overtime page you can pay fewer minutes or reject a day, and set when they left if they never scanned out. The hourly rate of monthly staff is one day's pay ÷ the shift's hours; of daily staff, the daily rate ÷ the shift's hours.
