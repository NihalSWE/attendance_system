Copy departments

A department belongs to one branch, because each branch has its own head, its own opening dates and its own device rules for it. A new branch therefore starts empty. This creates its own copies of another branch's active departments and their designations, so you do not have to add them one at a time.

Heads are not copied — the head of a department at one branch is rarely the right person at another, so each copy starts without one.
