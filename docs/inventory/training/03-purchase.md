# Purchase

**Every day:** turn requisitions into orders, get them approved, send them to
suppliers, follow up what has not come.

| Task | Where | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| Ask to buy | Purchase → Requisitions → New | Branch, department, lines → Send for approval |
| Order | Purchase → Purchase orders → New (or From requisitions) | Supplier, branch, dates, terms → lines with price, discount, VAT → **Submit** |
| Approve | Purchase → Purchase orders (Waiting) | **Approve** - a different person than who made it - or Send back with a note |
| Send | The approved order | **PDF** / **Email to supplier** |
| What is low | Inventory → Reports → Reorder report (and the morning alert) | Order what is below its reorder point |

**Watch:** an order can only be cancelled before goods come in; after that,
**Close** it with the reason the rest will not come.
