# Managers and accounts

| Task | Where | When |
|---|---|---|
| Stock at a glance | Inventory → Dashboard | Daily |
| Low stock, expiry | The morning email; Inventory → Reports → Reorder, Batch and expiry | Daily |
| Approve adjustments and counts | Inventory → Adjustments / Stock counts (Waiting) | As they come |
| Stock value | Inventory → Reports → Stock valuation (any past date) | Month-end, on request |
| Purchases, sales, margins | Reports → Purchase register, Sales register, Customer and item sales | Weekly |
| Production cost and yield | Reports → Production yield and wastage | Weekly |
| Close the month | Inventory → Month-end close | By the 5th of the next month |

**Month-end:** the page lists documents dated in the month that are not
posted - post, cancel or re-date them - and the stock check must agree. Close;
download the valuation at the month's last day for the books. A closed month
takes no back-dated entries; corrections go in the open month.
