# Department head

**Every day:** ask the store for what the department needs, approve what your
people ask for.

| Task | Where | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| Ask the store | Inventory → Store requisitions → New | Department, store, needed by → lines → **Send for approval** |
| Approve | Inventory → Store requisitions (Waiting for approval) | Open → **Approve** (or Reject with a note) |
| Follow up | The requisition | Partly issued / issued; close it if the rest is not needed |

You cannot approve your own requisition - someone else with "Approve store
requisitions" must.
